Privacy Policy

AgeTrack — Age & Date Insights App

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the AgeTrack mobile application ("AgeTrack", "the App", "we", "us") handles information. AgeTrack is built to work entirely on your device, and we designed it from the ground up to collect as little as possible — in fact, nothing at all.

In short: AgeTrack does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • No internet connection is used by the app
  • No account, sign-up, or login is required
  • No advertisements or ad networks
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No runtime permissions are requested

1. Information We Collect

AgeTrack does not collect any personal information, device identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, or usage analytics. The only piece of information the app works with is the date of birth (and any other dates) you choose to enter, which is used solely to perform on-device calculations such as your exact age, next birthday countdown, and date differences.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

Any date you enter is kept only in the app's temporary in-memory state while the app is open, purely to display results on screen. It is never written to a server, never uploaded to the internet, and never shared with any third party, because AgeTrack has no network or internet permission at all. When you close the app, this in-memory information is cleared.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

AgeTrack does not display advertisements and does not integrate any advertising network, analytics platform, or other third-party SDK that could collect or process your data. There are no third-party services involved in running this app.

5. Children's Privacy

Because AgeTrack collects no personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children either. The app is safe for use by a general audience.

6. Data Security

Since no personal data ever leaves your device or is stored anywhere outside of it, there is no server-side data to secure. All calculations happen locally using your device's own processor and clock.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is ever updated — for example, if a future version of AgeTrack adds a new feature — the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised, and the updated policy will be published at this same URL.
